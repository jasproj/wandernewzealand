Fiordland & Te Anau Tours

The gateway town for Milford and Doubtful Sounds: glowworm caves across the lake, heli-flights over the fiords, and the start of the Kepler and Milford Tracks.

About Te Anau and Fiordland

Te Anau is the town on the edge of Fiordland National Park and the sensible base for seeing both of the famous fiords without a 12-hour day from Queenstown. Milford Sound is 118 km up the Milford Road — two hours' drive, more with stops at Mirror Lakes, the Homer Tunnel and the Chasm. Doubtful Sound is reached from Manapouri, 20 minutes south, via a boat across Lake Manapouri and a bus over Wilmot Pass.

Te Anau's own attractions are the glowworm caves — a boat trip across the lake to a limestone cave system — and the start points of the Kepler Track (walkable as a day loop to the Luxmore Hut) and, via the Milford Road, the Routeburn and Milford Tracks. Helicopter operators based at Te Anau fly over the fiords, land on Mount Luxmore and reach Dusky Sound, which no road touches.

Everything here is further than it looks. Bus-and-cruise combinations from Te Anau remove the driving and are the standard way to do Milford in a day.

Best time to go: November to March

The Great Walks season — Milford, Routeburn, Kepler — opens in late October and runs to the end of April; this is when huts are serviced, bridges are in and the Milford Road is reliably open without avalanche closures. Day-cruise departures to both fiords move to their summer timetable in November, with the first Milford cruises leaving around 9am and the last in mid-afternoon. December to February is peak; November and March have the same daylight with easier bookings.

Fiordland gets 7 metres of rain a year and the waterfalls are at their best the day after a downpour. Bring a proper rain jacket in every month, and sandfly repellent from November on.

What to know before you book

  • Milford Sound is 2 hours from Te Anau by car — coach-cruise packages include the drive
  • Doubtful Sound trips leave from Manapouri (20 min south): boat, bus over Wilmot Pass, then cruise
  • Te Anau glowworm caves are a 2-hour evening or afternoon trip across the lake
  • Great Walks huts book out months ahead in summer; day walks on the Kepler need no booking
  • Heli-flights to Dusky Sound and Mount Luxmore weather-dependent — allow a spare morning
  • Sandflies are serious here from November — repellent, long sleeves

Fiordland questions

Should I stay in Te Anau or Queenstown for Milford Sound?

Te Anau if Milford is the priority. It cuts the return drive from about 8 hours (Queenstown) to 4, lets you take a morning cruise before the coach crowds, and puts Doubtful Sound and the glowworm caves within reach on other days.

Can you do both Milford and Doubtful Sound from Te Anau?

Yes, on consecutive days. Milford is a day trip up the Milford Road; Doubtful Sound is a separate full day from Manapouri. The two cannot be combined in one day by road — see our Milford vs Doubtful guide for how they differ.

How far is it from Milford Sound to Doubtful Sound?

About 40 km apart as the crow flies, but there is no road between them. By car it is roughly 4 hours back via Te Anau to Manapouri, then the lake crossing and Wilmot Pass bus. Helicopter is the only direct route.

Are the Te Anau glowworm caves worth it?

Yes if you have an evening free. The trip is a 30-minute lake crossing, a guided walk into the Aurora cave system and a short boat ride in the dark under the glowworms, about 2 hours 15 minutes in total.
