Fiordland Cruises

Milford and Doubtful Sound day cruises, overnight boats, and the coach and flight combinations that get you there from Queenstown and Te Anau — with live prices.

Choosing a Fiordland cruise

Fiordland National Park has fourteen fiords and two with scheduled cruises: Milford Sound and Doubtful Sound. Milford is the famous one — Mitre Peak, Stirling Falls, a 16 km run to the sea — and has the widest choice of boats, from 2-hour nature cruises to small-boat and overnight options. Doubtful is larger, quieter and reached only via Lake Manapouri and Wilmot Pass, which makes it a longer day and a stronger case for staying the night on board.

Prices depend less on the boat than on how you reach it. A Milford Sound cruise alone is the cheapest line on this page; add a coach from Te Anau and it roughly doubles; add a coach from Queenstown and the day becomes 12 hours; replace the return coach with a scenic flight (coach-cruise-fly) or fly both ways (fly-cruise-fly) and you are into the premium bracket but back in Queenstown by early afternoon.

Overnight cruises on either fiord include dinner, breakfast, kayaking and tender trips and anchor in arms the day boats never reach. For multi-day expedition cruising into Dusky, Breaksea and the southern fiords, a handful of small vessels run 3- to 7-night itineraries in summer.

Cruise season: November to March

Day cruises run every day of the year; what changes in summer is frequency and daylight. From November, Milford operators run departures roughly hourly from 9am to 3pm, overnight boats are on their full schedule, and the Milford Road is open without avalanche-control closures. Fly-cruise-fly and coach-cruise-fly packages depend on Milford airstrip weather and are most reliable from December to March.

Book overnight cabins and anything departing between 24 December and 31 January as early as you can; day cruises from Milford itself rarely sell out, but the coaches from Queenstown do.

What to know before you book

  • Milford Sound: 2 hours from Te Anau, 4 from Queenstown by road — cruise alone is 1.5–2.5 hours
  • Doubtful Sound: via Manapouri lake crossing + Wilmot Pass coach — about 7 hours total
  • Coach-cruise-fly and fly-cruise-fly packages cut the Queenstown day from 12 hours to 4–6
  • Overnight cruises include meals, kayaks and tender boats; cabins book out in summer
  • Prices shown are per adult in NZ$ where the operator publishes a per-person rate
  • Flights are weather-dependent; the coach is the fallback on cloudy days

Fiordland cruise questions

How much does a Fiordland cruise cost?

A Milford Sound day cruise alone starts around NZ$130–200 per adult. Coach-and-cruise from Te Anau is roughly NZ$250–400; from Queenstown NZ$290–520. Fly-cruise-fly from Queenstown runs NZ$700–900. Doubtful Sound day cruises from Manapouri are mid-NZ$300s; overnight cruises on either fiord start around NZ$600–750 per person in shared cabins and more for private cabins. Live prices are on the cards above.

Milford Sound or Doubtful Sound — which cruise should I pick?

Milford for the iconic view and a day trip from Queenstown; Doubtful for scale, quiet and an overnight. Our Milford vs Doubtful guide breaks it down.

Are there multi-day Fiordland cruises?

Yes — one- and two-night overnight cruises on Milford and Doubtful Sound run most of the year, and a few expedition vessels offer 3- to 7-night itineraries into Dusky and Breaksea Sounds in summer. The expedition trips are small-group and priced accordingly.

Can you cruise Fiordland from Queenstown in a day?

Yes. Coach-cruise-coach to Milford is a 12-hour day; coach-cruise-fly is 8–9 hours; fly-cruise-fly about 4 hours. Doubtful Sound from Queenstown by coach is 12–13 hours.

What is the difference between a Milford Sound nature cruise and a scenic cruise?

Nature cruises are usually smaller boats, 2–2.5 hours, with more commentary and closer approaches to the walls and wildlife. Scenic cruises are the larger vessels on 1.5–2 hour loops. Both reach the Tasman Sea entrance in normal conditions.
