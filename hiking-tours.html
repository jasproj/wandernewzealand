Types of Tours

Tongariro Alpine Crossing

New Zealand's most famous day hike. Traverse volcanic terrain, emerald lakes, and steaming vents across 19.4 km of the Central Plateau.

Glacier Walk

Strap on crampons and explore Fox or Franz Josef Glacier with certified guides. Heli-hike options skip the valley walk.

Coastal Track

Abel Tasman, Kaikoura Peninsula, and Cathedral Cove — golden beaches, turquoise water, and native bush.

Rainforest & Bush Walk

Ancient podocarp forest in Fiordland, Waipoua kauri forest, and Rotorua's geothermal wonderland trails.

Where to Go

Tongariro National Park

Central North Island

Dual World Heritage Site. The Alpine Crossing runs between Mangatepopo and Ketetahi through active volcanic landscape.

Franz Josef Glacier

West Coast

12 km glacier descending through temperate rainforest. Heli-hikes land you directly on the ice.

Milford Track

Fiordland

The 'finest walk in the world' — 53.5 km over 4 days through valleys, past waterfalls, and over Mackinnon Pass.

Abel Tasman

Nelson/Tasman

Coastal track combining golden beaches with native bush. Water taxis let you hike one way and cruise back.

Mount Cook

Mackenzie

Hooker Valley Track leads to glacier-fed lake views beneath Aoraki. Easy 3-hour return suitable for families.

Rotorua

Bay of Plenty

Geothermal trails past boiling mud pools, silica terraces, and the Redwoods treewalk through towering California redwoods.

Tips

Book Tongariro Shuttles

No private car parking at both ends — book a shuttle from National Park village or Turangi well in advance.

Weather Changes Fast

Alpine conditions can shift from sun to snow in an hour. Carry layers, rain gear, and extra food regardless of forecast.

Hut Passes Required

Great Walk huts (Milford, Routeburn, Kepler) require DOC booking months ahead for peak season.

Start Early

Tongariro and popular day hikes get crowded by 9 AM. Starting at 6-7 AM means smaller groups and better photos.
