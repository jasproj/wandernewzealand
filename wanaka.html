Wānaka & Cardrona Tours

Queenstown's quieter neighbour: Lake Wānaka, Mount Aspiring National Park, Cardrona Valley horse treks and the highest-rated skydive in the South Island.

About Wānaka

Wānaka sits an hour north-east of Queenstown over the Crown Range, on the southern shore of its own lake with Mount Aspiring National Park filling the western horizon. It has the same adventure menu as Queenstown — skydiving, canyoning, jetboats, heli-flights, lake cruises — at roughly half the crowd. The town centre is a single lakefront strip; most operator pickups are from the i-SITE or the lakefront car park.

Mount Aspiring is the draw for walkers and flyers. Guided day walks go up the Matukituki Valley to the Rob Roy Glacier viewpoint; helicopter operators land on the glaciers around Tititea/Mount Aspiring itself; and the Blue Pools and Haast Pass are a 70-minute drive north for a half-day. Lake cruises run to Mou Waho Island, which has its own lake on top with an island in it.

Cardrona, 25 minutes up the Crown Range road, is a ski field in winter and in summer a mountain-bike park and horse-trekking valley with the historic Cardrona Hotel at the bottom.

Best time to go: November to March

Summer is the walking and lake season. The Matukituki Valley road to the Rob Roy track is unsealed with fords that are usually passable from November; guided walks handle the river crossings. Skydives and heli-flights run year-round but have the clearest air and longest operating windows in summer, with sunrise flights from about 6am in December. Lake water reaches 18 to 20°C by late January.

Cardrona's summer trails and horse treks open from around Labour Weekend (late October) and run to Easter. Early January is peak; late February and March are drier and quieter.

What to know before you book

  • Wānaka is 60–70 minutes from Queenstown via the Crown Range (steep; chains in winter only)
  • Skydive and heli operators meet at Wānaka Airport, 10 minutes east of town
  • Rob Roy Glacier walk: 3–4 hours return, 50-minute gravel drive from town — guided tours include transport
  • Lake cruises and kayak hire leave from the lakefront by the i-SITE
  • Cardrona horse treks and bike park are 25 minutes up the Crown Range road
  • Alpine weather changes fast — wear layers for anything above the valley floor

Wānaka questions

Is Wānaka or Queenstown better for skydiving?

Both are world-renowned drop zones and both have excellent safety records. Wānaka's selling point is the view: Mount Aspiring, Lake Wānaka and Lake Hāwea from 15,000 feet, with fewer other aircraft in the sky. Queenstown's is the convenience if you are based there.

How do I get to the Rob Roy Glacier?

Drive 50 minutes up the Matukituki Valley on gravel (fords included) to Raspberry Creek car park, then walk 3–4 hours return. Guided walks from Wānaka include the drive and the river crossings, which is the sensible option without a 4WD.

Can you visit Cardrona in summer?

Yes. The ski field runs a summer mountain-bike park and chairlift from December to April; the Cardrona Valley has horse treks and the historic hotel year-round.

Where is the Wānaka tree?

On the lakeshore at the western end of Roys Bay, a 10-minute walk from the town centre along the lakefront path. Early morning is quietest.
