Wellington Tours

The capital on a harbour: Zealandia's kiwi, Weta Workshop, Middle-earth film locations, craft beer and the ferry to the South Island.

About Wellington

Wellington is compact enough to walk and built on hills steep enough that most visitors don't. The waterfront, Te Papa, the Cable Car and Cuba Street are all within 20 minutes of each other on foot; the tour scene fills in what's harder to reach on your own — Zealandia's predator-free valley five minutes from downtown, Weta Workshop in Miramar, the Lord of the Rings locations scattered through the Hutt Valley and the town belt, and the islands in the harbour.

Harbour trips run to Matiu/Somes Island, a former quarantine station now managed as a reserve, and on calmer days out to the heads and the south coast seal colonies. Kayak and SUP hire operates from the waterfront. Food and craft-beer walking tours lean on the density of Wellington's bar and café scene; most run three hours and stop at four or five venues.

North of the city, Kāpiti Island is one of the country's most important bird sanctuaries and takes a permitted boat crossing from Paraparaumu, about 50 minutes up the coast.

Best time to go: November to March

Wellington's wind is real, but summer is when the harbour is most often flat enough for kayaks, SUPs and the Somes Island ferry to run every scheduled sailing. Zealandia's night tours — the best way to see a kiwi in the wild — run year-round but start later in summer (around 9pm in December) because of the long twilight. Kāpiti Island crossings are most reliable from November onwards.

Late February to early March hosts the Fringe Festival and CubaDupa; accommodation tightens and walking tours book out on the weekends.

What to know before you book

  • Zealandia is 10 minutes from the CBD by free shuttle from the i-SITE; night tours need booking
  • Weta Workshop tours are in Miramar — 20 minutes by bus or included in film-location tours
  • Somes Island ferries run from Queens Wharf; last return sailing is mid-afternoon
  • Kāpiti Island requires a licensed operator; check the sea-state cancellation policy
  • The Interislander and Bluebridge ferries to Picton leave from different terminals
  • Wind cancellations are common for harbour kayaking — operators reschedule freely

Wellington questions

Can you see a kiwi in Wellington?

Yes — Zealandia's night tour is the most reliable wild kiwi sighting in any New Zealand city. Little spotted kiwi were reintroduced to the fenced valley and are heard, and usually seen, on most evening tours.

Which Lord of the Rings locations are near Wellington?

Mount Victoria (the hobbits hiding from the Nazgûl), the Hutt River (Anduin), Kaitoke Regional Park (Rivendell) and Harcourt Park (Isengard gardens). Half-day and full-day tours cover the town-belt sites and the Hutt Valley; most add a Weta Workshop visit.

Is Matiu/Somes Island worth visiting?

For a half-day, yes. The ferry takes 20 minutes, the island loop walk is about an hour, and you will see tuatara, weta and little blue penguins depending on the season. Take food; there is nothing to buy on the island.

How do I get to Kāpiti Island?

By licensed boat from Paraparaumu Beach, 50 minutes north of Wellington by train or car. Day permits are included with the operator booking; the crossing is 15 minutes and sailings depend on the swell.
