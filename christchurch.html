Christchurch Tours

Punting on the Avon, Hector's dolphins at Akaroa, penguins on Banks Peninsula, and the TranzAlpine corridor into the Southern Alps.

About Christchurch

Christchurch is the South Island's largest city and the arrival point for most travellers heading to Kaikōura, Aoraki/Mount Cook or the West Coast. The city has rebuilt around the Avon River, Hagley Park and the new Riverside precinct, and the tour scene reflects that: punting, tram tours, guided walks of the post-earthquake architecture, and street-art bike rides.

Banks Peninsula, 80 minutes south-east, is where the wildlife is. Akaroa Harbour is a drowned volcanic crater and home to Hector's dolphins, the world's smallest marine dolphin; harbour cruises and dolphin swims run daily from the Akaroa wharf in summer. Over the hill at Flea Bay, the Pōhatu white-flippered penguin colony is visited by 4WD and sea-kayak safaris that leave from Akaroa village.

Inland, the Waimakariri Gorge jetboats and the Castle Hill limestone are an hour out on the Arthur's Pass road, and day trips to Lake Tekapo and Mount Cook run from the city for those not hiring a car.

Best time to go: November to March

Summer is when Akaroa works best. Hector's dolphins are in the harbour year-round but come closer inshore from November to March, and the swim-with-dolphins permits are capped per boat, so those trips sell out first. Penguin numbers at Pōhatu peak during the September-to-February breeding season; by late February the adults are moulting and sightings drop.

Christchurch's nor'wester can shut down Akaroa boat trips at short notice. Most operators will move you to the next available departure, so leave a spare morning if a dolphin swim is the priority.

What to know before you book

  • Akaroa is 80 minutes from the city — many wildlife tours include Christchurch pickup
  • Dolphin swim permits limit numbers per boat; book ahead for December–February
  • Pōhatu penguin tours leave from Akaroa village, not the wharf — check the meeting point
  • The nor'wester cancels harbour trips; keep a spare morning
  • Punting and tram tours run from the central city year-round
  • Arthur's Pass and Castle Hill are a day trip; Mount Cook is a long one (5 hours each way)

Christchurch questions

Can you swim with dolphins in Christchurch?

Not in the city, but at Akaroa on Banks Peninsula, 80 minutes away. Licensed operators run swims with Hector's dolphins from the Akaroa wharf; wetsuits and snorkels are supplied and numbers in the water are capped by permit.

What is the best day trip from Christchurch?

Akaroa for wildlife (dolphins, penguins, harbour cruise), Arthur's Pass or Castle Hill for alpine scenery, Kaikōura for whales (2.5 hours north). Lake Tekapo and Aoraki/Mount Cook are possible in a long day but better as an overnight.

How far is Akaroa from Christchurch?

About 75 km, 1 hour 20 minutes by road over the Hilltop. Several operators include return transfers from central Christchurch hotels.

When do the penguins come ashore at Pōhatu?

Through the breeding season from roughly September to February. Evening tours time arrival for the birds returning after dark; daytime tours visit the nest boxes and the marine reserve.
