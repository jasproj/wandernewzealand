Types of Tours

Beginner Surf Lesson

Stand up on your first session at gentle beach breaks. Instructors provide boards, wetsuits, and patient coaching in waist-deep water.

Intermediate Coaching

Progress beyond the whitewash. Learn to read sets, paddle into green waves, and turn on NZ's consistent point breaks.

Surf Safari

Multi-day road trips chasing swell along the Waikato, Taranaki, or East Cape coastlines. Guides know the hidden spots.

Stand-Up Paddleboard

SUP tours on flat water — harbours, estuaries, and river mouths. A gentler way to get on the water when the surf is flat.

Where to Go

Raglan

Waikato

NZ's most famous surf town. Manu Bay and Indicators deliver long, consistent left-hand point breaks. Beginner-friendly Ngarunui Beach nearby.

Mount Maunganui

Bay of Plenty

Year-round surf with warm water by NZ standards. The Mount's beach breaks suit all levels, with cafes right on the sand.

Piha

Auckland

Auckland's wild west coast icon. Powerful black-sand beach break with Lion Rock as the backdrop. Strong currents — respect the flags.

Taranaki

Taranaki

40+ breaks around Mount Taranaki's coastline. Stent Road, Kumara Patch, and Back Beach offer variety in every swell direction.

Dunedin

Otago

Cold water, empty lineups, and punchy beach breaks at St Clair and St Kilda. Thick wetsuits required year-round.

Gisborne

East Cape

First city in the world to see the sunrise — and the first to catch the morning swell. Wainui Beach is the local favourite.

Tips

Wetsuit is Essential

Water temperatures range from 13C (south, winter) to 22C (north, summer). A 3/2mm suit is minimum; 4/3mm for the South Island.

Respect the Locals

NZ surf culture values courtesy. Wait your turn, don't drop in, and a friendly nod goes a long way in the lineup.

Swim Between the Flags

Rip currents are real, especially at west coast beaches. Surf near lifeguard-patrolled areas until you know the break.

Check MetService

metservice.com gives accurate swell forecasts by region. A solid 1.5m SW swell lights up most west coast spots.
