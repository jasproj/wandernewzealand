Types of Tours

Kaituna Waterfall Raft

Plunge 7 metres over Tutea Falls — the world's highest commercially rafted waterfall. Grade 5 thrills near Rotorua in just 50 minutes on the water.

Shotover Canyon Raft

Queenstown's signature adrenaline hit. Grade 3-5 rapids through a narrow, gold-mining-era canyon beneath a 100-metre-deep gorge.

Scenic Grade 2-3 Float

Relaxed rafting for families and beginners. The Tongariro, Buller, and Rangitikei rivers offer gentle rapids with stunning scenery.

Multi-Day Expedition

Overnight trips on the Mohaka, Rangitikei, or Landsborough rivers. Camp riverside, cook over fire, and raft through true backcountry.

Where to Go

Kaituna River

Rotorua

14 rapids in 50 minutes including the 7m Tutea Falls. Short, intense, and unforgettable — NZ's most popular raft run.

Shotover River

Queenstown

170m deep canyon with Grade 3-5 rapids. A 4WD shuttle through Skippers Canyon is half the adventure.

Tongariro River

Taupo

Grade 3 rapids beneath volcanic peaks. Also famous for trout fishing — you might spot anglers between rapids.

Rangitata Gorge

Canterbury

Grade 5 rapids through a gorge flanked by alpine tussock. Includes a natural hot pool stop in the river.

Buller River

West Coast

Grade 3-4 rapids through native beech forest. The Earthquake Section delivers NZ's longest commercially rafted rapid.

Mohaka River

Hawke's Bay

Remote backcountry river with Grade 3-4 rapids, gorge swimming, and overnight camping on a gravel beach.

Tips

No Experience Needed

Grade 3 and most Grade 5 trips require zero prior rafting. Guides run a safety briefing and do the steering — you just paddle.

Bring a Change of Clothes

You will get soaked. Leave dry clothes and a towel in the car. Operators provide wetsuits and splash jackets.

Minimum Age Varies

Kaituna and Shotover are 13+. Family-friendly Grade 2 trips often take kids as young as 5.

Go in Spring

October-November brings snowmelt-fed rivers at peak flow with fewer crowds than the Dec-Feb summer rush.
