Kaikōura Tours

Sperm whales a few kilometres offshore year-round, dusky dolphins in pods of hundreds, and fur seals you can paddle a kayak past.

About Kaikōura

Kaikōura sits where the Seaward Kaikōura Range drops into the Pacific and a 1,000-metre-deep submarine canyon runs almost to the beach. That canyon is why resident sperm whales feed here all year within a 20-minute boat ride of the harbour, and why Kaikōura built a whole town's economy on watching them. Whale trips run by catamaran from South Bay and by fixed-wing aircraft and helicopter from the airfield.

Dusky dolphins are the other headline. Pods of several hundred live off the peninsula and the swim-with-dolphins operators put you in the water with them on early-morning trips; wetsuits and snorkels are supplied. New Zealand fur seals haul out at Point Kean on the peninsula walkway and along the coast north of town, and guided sea-kayak trips paddle along the colony at close range.

Kaikōura is 2.5 hours north of Christchurch and 2 hours south of Blenheim on State Highway 1, with the Coastal Pacific train stopping in summer.

Best time to go: November to March

Sperm whales are resident, so whale watching is good year-round; summer adds calmer seas, fewer cancellations and, from December, the chance of orca, blue and humpback whales passing through. Dusky dolphin swims run all year but the water is warmest (16–18°C) from January to March, and the dolphins are most inshore in summer. Seal pups are on the rocks from December; kayak trips keep the required distance but you will see plenty.

Early-morning departures (6:30–7:30am) have the flattest sea and the best chance of a full trip. Afternoon trips in summer can be cancelled by the sea breeze — book the morning and keep the afternoon free.

What to know before you book

  • Whale watching leaves from South Bay (boat) or the airfield (plane/heli), both 5 minutes from town
  • Boat trips are 3–3.5 hours; seasickness tablets are worth taking
  • Dolphin swims are early morning — wetsuits, masks and snorkels supplied
  • Seal kayaking paddles past the Point Kean colony; no experience needed
  • The peninsula walkway (3 hours) is free and passes the main seal haul-out
  • SH1 north and south can close in heavy rain — check before a day trip from Christchurch

Kaikōura questions

What is the best time of year to see whales in Kaikōura?

Sperm whales are here every month, so any time works. December to March adds calmer seas and migrating humpbacks and orca; June to August is the season for the largest sperm whale bulls. Morning trips have the best conditions.

Can you swim with dolphins in Kaikōura?

Yes. Licensed operators run early-morning trips to swim with dusky dolphins in the open sea; pods often number in the hundreds. Snorkelling ability is required; wetsuits are supplied and numbers in the water are limited by permit.

How far is Kaikōura from Christchurch?

About 180 km, 2.5 hours by road on SH1, or 3 hours on the Coastal Pacific train (summer timetable). It works as a long day trip but an overnight gives you the morning dolphin swim.

Is whale watching by boat or by plane better?

The boat gets you close to the whale at the surface and lasts 3 hours; the plane sees the whole animal underwater from above and takes 30 to 40 minutes. The boat is more affected by swell; the plane by cloud.
