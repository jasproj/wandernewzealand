About Tauranga and the Mount

Tauranga is the country's biggest export port and its fastest-growing city, but the visitor centre of gravity is Mount Maunganui — a beach suburb wrapped around Mauao, the 232-metre volcanic cone at the harbour entrance. The Mauao base track takes 45 minutes; the summit an hour. Below it, the main beach is one of the most consistent learner surf breaks in the North Island, and surf schools run from the sand.

Offshore, common and bottlenose dolphins work the Bay of Plenty most days, and wildlife cruises leave from Tauranga Bridge Marina and Salisbury Wharf. Inland, McLaren Falls Park has a glow-worm gully that kayak operators paddle after dark, and the Kaimai Range offers waterfall walks within 30 minutes. Hobbiton at Matamata is 50 minutes west and is a standard day trip from here.

Cruise ships berth at the Mount from October to April, which means the dolphin and Hobbiton tours fill on port days; check the schedule.

Best time to go: November to March

The Mount is a summer beach town. Water temperature passes 20°C by January, surf schools run multiple sessions a day, and the dolphin operators go to daily departures. December 26 to the end of January is busiest — the Mount's population roughly triples — and the glow-worm kayak tours, which take small groups, sell out well ahead on those dates. November and March have the same water, less crowd.

Evening kayak tours start later in summer (around 8:30pm) to allow full dark for the glow-worms.

What to know before you book

  • Mount Maunganui and Tauranga are 10 minutes apart across the harbour bridge
  • Dolphin cruises depart from Tauranga Bridge Marina — allow 4–5 hours
  • Surf lessons run from the main beach at the Mount; boards and wetsuits included
  • Glow-worm kayaking at McLaren Falls is 20 minutes inland and runs after dark
  • Hobbiton is 50 minutes west — several tours include Tauranga pickup
  • Cruise-ship days fill tours; book ahead for October–April port days

Tauranga questions

Can you swim with dolphins in Tauranga?

Licensed wildlife cruises from Tauranga encounter common and bottlenose dolphins on most summer trips and, where conditions and permits allow, offer in-water time. Sightings are not guaranteed; most operators offer a return trip if none are seen.

Is Mount Maunganui good for learning to surf?

Yes — the main beach is a sandy-bottom beach break with gentle whitewater in the inside and schools operating from the sand with all gear supplied. Lessons run year-round; summer water is warmest.

Where is the glow-worm kayaking near Tauranga?

McLaren Falls Park, about 20 minutes south-west of the city, where guided kayak tours paddle Lake McLaren into a glow-worm canyon after dark. Tours run most evenings in summer and take small groups.

How far is Hobbiton from Tauranga?

About 55 km to Matamata, 50 minutes by road. Hobbiton tours with Tauranga hotel or cruise-terminal pickup are widely available.
