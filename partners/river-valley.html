The operation

Rafting from 1982, a lodge from 1987, four generations

The rafting came first, in 1982. The lodge followed five years later, and the operation has stayed in the same family through four generations since. It sits in the Rangitikei gorge well off the highway, which is why most people who raft here also sleep here.

The river runs the full range. Grade 2 water is gentle enough for families; the Grade 5 day is a serious piece of whitewater through a deep papa-rock gorge. The company holds AdventureMark certification and a Qualmark Gold rating, and runs an on-site conservation programme for the whio, the blue duck, along the river corridor. Reviewers are near-unanimous about the food, which comes largely out of the lodge garden.

The one to book

Grade 5 Whitewater Rafting on the Rangitikei

The full Grade 5 run through the gorge, with guides who have worked this water for decades. Wetsuit, helmet and buoyancy aid provided; no experience required, but this is the hard version of the river.

Most people pair it with a night at the lodge. The stay-and-raft package starts at NZ$445 per person on a bunk bed and includes the rafting, dinner and breakfast.

Before you go

Know before you go

Getting there

The valley is well off State Highway 1 near Taihape, down an unsealed road. Allow more time than the map suggests.

Food

Dinner and breakfast are included on the stay packages and come largely from the lodge garden. It is the single most praised thing in reviews.

Grades

Grade 2 family water through to a full Grade 5 day. Say which you want when you book.

Accreditation

AdventureMark certified and Qualmark Gold rated.

Reviewers praise the guides by name, the food from the lodge garden and the family hospitality. There is no recurring complaint; the only notes are that the gravel access road takes practice on a bike and that one trip was rerouted around a landslide.

Questions

Good to know

Where is it?

In the Rangitikei gorge near Taihape, off State Highway 1 down an unsealed road.

What does it cost?

NZ$280 per adult for the Grade 5 run, or from NZ$445 per person with a night and meals. Checked October 2, 2026.

Do I need rafting experience?

No. The river runs from Grade 2 family water to a full Grade 5 day, so say which you want.

What is the blue duck programme?

An on-site conservation effort protecting whio, a rare native duck, along the river corridor.
