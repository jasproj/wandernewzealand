The operation

Owner-run, award-winning, and priced for small parties

The company is run by Neill and Ceillhe Sperath out of Auckland and sells two things: small-group scheduled tours with a published per-adult fare, and private tours priced for the whole party. In 2016 it won the National Geographic World Legacy Award for Sense of Place, presented with ITB Berlin — an award for tourism that reflects the place it happens in rather than flattening it, which is the fairest summary of what the tours actually do.

The Māori content is the distinguishing feature. The Māori Luxury Tour and its variants include marae visits and cultural interpretation through the day rather than a performance bolted onto the end, and reviewers single this out more than any other element. Guides are called host-guides, vehicles are Mercedes, and the private itineraries are genuinely rearranged around what guests want rather than offered as a fixed route.

Multi-day private tours run from two days taking in Waitomo, Hobbiton and Rotorua up to a seven-day Auckland to Wellington route, and one-way private transfers between Auckland and Rotorua can be booked with Hobbiton or Waitomo built into the drive. Infants travel free on the private tours.

Before you go

Know before you go

Small group or private

The published per-adult fares are the scheduled small-group tours. Private tours are priced for the party and the per-head figure drops sharply from one person to five.

Where the cultural content sits

On the Māori Luxury Tour and its private version it runs through the whole day, including marae visits. It is the single most praised element in reviews.

Seating on shared tours

The one recurring criticism is from a guest seated at the back of a shared half-day tour who felt overlooked. If seating matters, ask when you book or take a private tour.

Children and infants

Children are half price on the small-group tours. Infants travel free on the private tours.

Reviewers praise the depth of the guiding and the Māori cultural content above everything else, then the flexibility of the private itineraries and the comfort of the vehicles. The price is premium and reviewers do not treat that as a fault.
