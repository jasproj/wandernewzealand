The operation

Four beaches, one method, and a promise it keeps

The school runs from Te Arai and Mangawhai in Northland, Orewa on Auckland’s Hibiscus Coast and Sumner Beach in Christchurch. Te Arai is the one it talks about most: an undeveloped stretch of white sand with changing rooms and toilets and, by the school’s account, the most consistent conditions of the four. Lessons move with the swell, so the exact spot can change on the day.

The two-hour beginner lesson covers safety, board design, paddling and positioning, the pop-up and then riding waves, and the school states plainly that every surfer will be standing and riding by the end of it. Ages six and up. Beyond the single lesson there are three-day school-holiday programmes, after-school squads, a four-week women’s programme called Surf Sistas, kids’ birthday parties and board and wetsuit hire at each beach.

Before you go

Know before you go

Minimum numbers

The Te Arai and Mangawhai two-hour lessons require at least two people. Orewa will take a single surfer.

The group rate is real

Book as a family or group at Te Arai or Mangawhai and the per-person price drops from NZ$79 to NZ$69. Three lessons together are NZ$199.

Conditions move the lesson

The school adjusts beaches and times with the swell. This is the only real caveat in the reviews and it is a feature, not a fault.

What is provided

Boards and wetsuits. Bring swimwear, a towel and sunscreen.
