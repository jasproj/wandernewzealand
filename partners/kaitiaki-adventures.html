The operation

Guardianship, and a seven-metre drop

The company dates itself to 1999 and describes its founding as a mix of adventure professionals and local entrepreneurs built on manaakitanga and kaitiakitanga. It explains the name on its own About page: the basic meaning of tiaki is to guard, but it also means to preserve, keep, conserve, nurture, protect and watch over. Guides come from Te Arawa, Ngāti Rangitihi, Ngāpuhi and other iwi, and the company lists more than twenty-six of them with senior raft, sledge and mountain guide grades.

The Kaituna run drops over Tutea Falls, which the company and its reviewers both describe as the highest commercially rafted waterfall in the world at seven metres. Reviewers consistently mention a karakia offered to the river before the trip begins. Separately, Kaitiaki holds the sole concession to take manuhiri to the summit of Mount Tarawera, the volcano that erupted in 1886. The company states it is 120 percent climate positive.

The one to book

Raft the Kaituna River

About three hours from the Okere Falls base, with roughly an hour on the water. Fourteen rapids on a Grade 5 run through native bush, finishing with the seven-metre drop over Tutea Falls. Wetsuit, helmet, buoyancy aid and guide included.

No rafting experience needed and the minimum age is 13. Reviewers routinely name the falls as the moment that made the trip.

Before you go

Know before you go

Departure

Okere Falls for the river trips; Mount Tarawera trips depart Rotorua. Confirm your pick-up when you book.

What to bring

Togs and a towel for the river. Wetsuit, helmet and buoyancy aid are provided.

Minimum age

13 for the Grade 5 Kaituna run. The crater walk and the combos have their own limits.

Before you book

Reviewers have reported charges at checkout they did not expect. Check the total on the booking page before you pay.

Reviewers praise the guides by name, the safety briefings and the cultural grounding of the trips, and single out the seven-metre waterfall. The recurring complaint is fees at checkout that guests had not expected; a smaller number mention restrictions on helmet-mounted cameras.

Questions

Good to know

Where does the rafting leave from?

Okere Falls, about twenty minutes north of Rotorua.

What does it cost?

NZ$146.25 per rafter on the Kaituna. Checked October 2, 2026.

How high is the waterfall?

Seven metres. The company and its reviewers both describe it as the highest commercially rafted waterfall in the world.

Can anyone climb Mount Tarawera?

Only with Kaitiaki. The company holds the sole concession for guided access to the summit.
