The operation

A farm, a trapline, and a penguin that lives almost nowhere else

The white-flippered kōrorā is a subspecies of little penguin found almost entirely around Banks Peninsula, and Pōhatu — Flea Bay — holds the largest mainland colony of them in Australasia. Francis and Shireen Helps farm the bay and have run predator control here for more than thirty years, with around forty per cent of the property under covenant and a charitable trust, Kōrorā Kaitiakitanga, carrying the work.

Getting there is part of it: the bay sits behind the crater rim above Akaroa and the tours run in by 4WD over the farm track. Alongside the penguin tours there is sea kayaking under the cliffs, a half-day scenic nature safari, and accommodation in the bay for people walking the Banks Track. The operation is Qualmark Gold, DOC-approved and certified by Sustainable Tourism Aotearoa.

Before you go

Know before you go

Penguins are wild

The most common criticism in reviews is that the birds were distant or had not come ashore during the tour window. The operation says plainly that sightings are not guaranteed and that tours leave before dark in breeding season to limit disturbance. Both of those are the right call and both can leave you without a close view.

Getting in

The bay is behind the crater rim from Akaroa, down a farm 4WD track. Take the transport unless you know the road.

What you are paying for

Thirty years of predator control, roughly forty per cent of the property covenanted, and a charitable trust. The tour funds the trapline.

Accreditation

Qualmark Gold, DOC-approved, certified by Sustainable Tourism Aotearoa.

Questions

Good to know

Where is it?

Pōhatu, Flea Bay, over the crater rim from Akaroa on Banks Peninsula.

What does it cost?

NZ$110.48 per adult for the two-hour nature tour, NZ$131.52 for the evening penguin tour. Checked October 2, 2026.

Will I definitely see penguins?

No. They are wild and the operation does not promise it. Evening tours give the best chance, and they still leave before dark in breeding season.

What makes this colony special?

It is the largest mainland colony of white-flippered little penguins in Australasia, a subspecies found almost only around Banks Peninsula.
