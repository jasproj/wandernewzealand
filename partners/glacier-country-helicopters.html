The operation

A family company on the only coast like this

Franz Josef is one of very few places on earth where a glacier descends through temperate rainforest to within a few kilometres of the sea. Westland Tai Poutini National Park sits against Aoraki/Mount Cook National Park, and the two together give a helicopter somewhere genuinely extraordinary to go in twenty-five minutes. Glacier Country Helicopters flies from the township itself, not from a distant airfield.

Every scenic flight includes a landing on snow, which is the part reviewers remember. The flights step up by duration rather than by destination: twenty-five minutes covers Franz Josef and the Southern Alps, thirty-five adds Fox, forty-five adds Tasman, fifty adds the Shackleton Glacier with a whisky tasting named after it, sixty reaches Mount Cook, and ninety goes into country the shorter flights never see. The company is family owned and reviewers consistently describe the handling of weather cancellations as fair, with full refunds.

Before you go

Know before you go

The “Comfort Seat” rate

Each flight lists a Comfort Seat at about half the adult fare — the same figure as the infant rate. Ask what it actually covers before you plan around it; the adult fare is the real price of a seat.

Middle seats

The most common complaint in reviews is an obstructed view from the middle seat, and that the offer to swap sides on the return leg is not always kept. If photographs matter, say so when you check in.

Weather and mechanicals

Cancellations happen at Franz Josef and reviewers report full refunds, but some also report late notification. Leave a spare day and give the office a phone number they can actually reach.

Snow landing

Included on every scenic flight listed above. It is the part people remember.
