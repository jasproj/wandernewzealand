Marlborough makes most of New Zealand’s sauvignon blanc, and the best of its cellar doors sit on flat roads within a few kilometres of each other. A bike is the obvious way around and almost nobody needs to be fit to do it. Three hours of hire is NZ$47.35; a guided morning with a self-guided afternoon is the version most people should book.

The operation

A dozen cellar doors inside three kilometres

The base is The Vines Village at 193 Rapaura Road, which is the single most useful fact about the operation: more than a dozen wineries sit within about three kilometres of it, on flat ground. Almost no time goes on transit, which is what makes a three-hour hire a real wine tour rather than a bike ride with one stop.

The range runs from pure hire to fully guided. Self-guided gets you the bike, a map and the bookings; guided puts someone with you who knows which cellar door is pouring what. The hybrid — guided in the morning, self-guided after lunch — is the one reviewers single out, because it gives you the expertise first and the freedom afterwards. The company is Qualmark Gold, a Carbon Conscious Organisation and a Tiaki Promise signatory, and says it is the only operator running guided biking wine tours in the region.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where it starts

The Vines Village, 193 Rapaura Road, with more than a dozen cellar doors inside about three kilometres.

Time is the real constraint

The recurring criticism in reviews is that the day feels rushed — people wanting longer at the wineries, and the scenic river route eating into tasting time. If that matters, take a full day rather than a half.

Not every stop lands

At least one winery on the standard route is described by reviewers as dull next to the others. Ask the guide which ones are pouring well on the day.

Bikes and e-bikes

Every price here is the standard bike rate. Electric bikes carry a surcharge on each tour.

Good to know

Where does it start?

The Vines Village, 193 Rapaura Road, between Blenheim and Renwick.

What does it cost?

NZ$47.35 for three hours of self-guided hire on a standard bike, up to NZ$436.65 for the deluxe guided half day. Checked October 2, 2026.

Do I need to be fit?

No. The riding is flat and the cellar doors are within about three kilometres of the base.

Guided or self-guided?

The hybrid — guided morning, self-guided afternoon, NZ$236.74 — is the one reviewers rate highest.
