Types of Tours

Milford Sound Cruise

Glide beneath towering fiord walls and cascading waterfalls on one of the world's most scenic waterways. Most cruises run 1.5-2.5 hours.

Bay of Islands Sailing

Island-hop through 144 subtropical islands on a catamaran or tall ship. Dolphins, snorkelling stops, and the famous Hole in the Rock.

Whale & Dolphin Watch

Kaikoura is world-renowned for sperm whale sightings year-round. Dusky dolphins, orcas, and Hector's dolphins also frequent NZ waters.

Jet Boat Adventure

Shotover Jet and Huka Falls jet boats deliver high-speed thrills through narrow canyons and crystal-clear rivers.

Where to Go

Milford Sound

Fiordland

Sheer granite peaks, Stirling Falls, and resident fur seals. Accessible via the scenic Milford Road from Te Anau.

Bay of Islands

Northland

144 islands, warm waters, and bottlenose dolphins. Cape Brett and the Hole in the Rock are the headline cruises.

Kaikoura

Canterbury

Year-round sperm whale sightings backed by the Kaikoura Ranges. Albatross and dolphin encounters too.

Auckland Harbour

Auckland

City-skyline cruises, Waiheke Island ferries, and America's Cup sailing experiences on the Hauraki Gulf.

Abel Tasman

Nelson/Tasman

Water taxi and kayak combos along golden-sand beaches inside the national park.

Akaroa

Canterbury

Volcanic harbour home to the world's smallest dolphin (Hector's). French colonial charm on shore.

Tips

Book Milford Early

Peak season (Dec-Feb) sells out weeks ahead. Morning departures get calmer water and better light.

Seasickness Prep

Kaikoura and offshore trips can be rough. Take medication 30 minutes before boarding, sit mid-vessel.

Layer Up

Fiordland water temperature hovers around 12C even in summer. Bring a windproof jacket for open decks.

Check Cancellations

Weather cancellations are common in Fiordland and Kaikoura. Most operators offer free rebooking or refunds.
