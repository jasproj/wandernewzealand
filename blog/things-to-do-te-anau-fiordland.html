13 Best Things to Do in Te Anau & Fiordland, New Zealand

Te Anau is the town at the edge of Fiordland National Park — 1.2 million hectares of rainforest, fiords and the wettest mountains in the country. It is the base for Milford Sound (2 hours up the most scenic road in New Zealand), Doubtful Sound (across Lake Manapouri), three Great Walks and a glowworm cave reached by boat. This guide covers 13 things to do and how to choose between the two fiords. Planning the trip? Start with our Fiordland & Te Anau tours for live prices from local operators.

The fiords

1. Cruise Milford Sound

Signature 1.5–2 hrs on the water

The fiord runs 15 km from the wharf to the Tasman Sea under Mitre Peak (1,692 m), with Stirling and Bowen Falls dropping straight into the water and fur seals on Seal Rock. Boats range from 400-seat vessels to small boutique launches; the small boats get under the waterfalls and closer to the seals. After rain — and Milford gets 7 metres a year — the walls run with hundreds of temporary falls. Two hours from Te Anau by road; coach-and-cruise packages handle the driving.

3. Cruise Doubtful Sound

Full day Quieter

Doubtful is three times longer than Milford, ten times larger, and reached in three stages: a boat across Lake Manapouri, a coach over Wilmot Pass, then a 3-hour cruise in the fiord with bottlenose dolphins, a possible penguin, and the engines switched off for silence at the Tasman end. An 8-hour day from Manapouri (20 minutes from Te Anau). Fewer boats, no road, no day-tripper crowds.

4. Overnight on Doubtful Sound

Overnight Kayaking included

The overnight cruise anchors in a sheltered arm for the night with kayaks and a tender off the stern, dinner on board, and the fiord to yourselves at dawn. Cabins range from quad-share bunks to private en-suite. It is the single best thing to do in Fiordland if you can spend the money and a night. Departs Manapouri; coach connections from Te Anau and Queenstown.

5. Milford or Doubtful — do both?

Planning 2 days

Yes, on separate days from Te Anau — there is no road between them. Milford is the dramatic one-day trip with the Milford Road drive; Doubtful is the longer, quieter day with more water and fewer boats. Our Milford vs Doubtful comparison goes into the detail. If you only have one day and came for the view, Milford; with a spare day, Doubtful overnight.

Te Anau and Lake Manapouri

6. Te Anau Glowworm Caves

Caves 2 hrs 15 min

A boat crosses Lake Te Anau to the western shore, then a guided walk follows an underground river through the Aurora cave system to a punt ride under a glowworm grotto, in silence and darkness. The caves are 'living' — the river is still cutting them. Runs several times daily; the evening departure gets the lake crossing at sunset.

7. Te Anau Bird Sanctuary

Free 30 min

On the lakefront 10 minutes' walk from town, DOC's Punanga Manu o Te Anau holds takahē — the flightless rail thought extinct until 1948 and rediscovered in the Murchison Mountains across the lake — plus kākā, kea and whio. It is free, open daily, and the best guaranteed takahē sighting in the country.

8. Walk the Kepler Track day section

Great Walk 4–6 hrs

The Kepler is the Great Walk that starts in Te Anau itself. A day on it means the 5.6-km lakeside walk from the control gates to Brod Bay (1.5 hours each way), or the climb from Brod Bay to Luxmore Hut (1,085 m, 3.5 hours up) for the view over the lake and the Murchisons. A water taxi to Brod Bay cuts the flat section. Hut and campsite bookings open each year for the October–April season.

9. Lake Manapouri and the Fraser's Beach walk

Free 1 hr

Manapouri is the deeper, more island-studded lake 20 km south of Te Anau, and the 1970 campaign to stop it being raised for the power station was the start of New Zealand's environmental movement. The Fraser's Beach track from Pearl Harbour is an easy 1-hour return along the shore. The hydro station itself can be visited on some Doubtful Sound tours.

The Milford Road

10. Mirror Lakes, the Chasm and Monkey Creek

Free Road stops

The 119-km Milford Road (State Highway 94) is the point, not just the drive. Stop at Mirror Lakes (5-minute boardwalk, 56 km), the Eglinton Valley viewpoint, Monkey Creek for kea and glacier-fed water, the Homer Tunnel (1.2 km, built 1935–53, one-way lights in summer), and the Chasm (20-minute walk) where the Cleddau River has cut smooth bowls into the rock. Allow 3 hours for the 2-hour drive.

11. Key Summit on the Routeburn Track

Walk 3 hrs return

From the Divide car park at the Milford end of the road, the Routeburn Track climbs for an hour to the Key Summit turnoff and a boardwalk loop over alpine tarns at 919 m, with views into three valleys and the Darran Mountains. The best short alpine walk on the road and a good leg stretch before or after a cruise.

12. Lake Marian

Walk 3 hrs return

Off the Hollyford Road just before the Homer Tunnel, a steep track through beech forest reaches a hanging-valley lake under the Darran peaks — one of the better half-day walks in the park. The first 20 minutes to the cascades is a gentle boardwalk; the climb beyond is rooty and wet. Not a wet-weather track.

13. Scenic flight over Fiordland

Flight 40 min–full day

Fixed-wing flights from Te Anau's airfield cross the Kepler Mountains and the Dusky and Doubtful fiords, or fly up to Milford for a cruise and back — the only way to see the southern fiords and Dusky Sound without a multi-day expedition boat. Weather-dependent; mornings are more reliable.

Best time to go: November to March

November to March is the window for everything here: the Kepler, Routeburn and Milford Track huts are open and bookable, the Milford Road is clear of avalanche closures, and days are long enough to drive the road with stops and still cruise. Expect rain — it is the reason the waterfalls exist. December and January are the busiest on the Milford wharf; Doubtful stays quiet.

Te Anau & Fiordland questions

How many days do you need in Te Anau?

Three days covers Milford Sound (a full day), Doubtful Sound (a full day) and a half day for the glowworm caves plus a Kepler or Milford Road walk. Two days if you choose one fiord.

Is Te Anau or Queenstown the better base for Milford Sound?

Te Anau. It is 2 hours from Milford versus 4 from Queenstown, so you can leave later, stop along the Milford Road and still make a midday cruise. Doubtful Sound departs from Manapouri, 20 minutes from Te Anau.

Can you do Milford Sound and Doubtful Sound in one day?

Not practically — different departure points (Milford wharf vs Manapouri), no road between them, and each is a full day. Do them on consecutive days from Te Anau, or combine a Milford day with a Doubtful overnight.

What is the best time to visit Fiordland?

November to March. Longer daylight for the Milford Road, the Great Walks huts open (late October to April), the Homer Tunnel runs without avalanche closures, and the waterfalls are still full. It rains year-round — 200 days a year at Milford — so a wet day is normal, not a failure.
